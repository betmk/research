### US Diesel (Ch 3) — STRUCTURAL CALL
- Extrapolating current diesel draw rate → US disty inventories run into trouble end of May.
- Commercial crude curve less desperate due to SPR cushion, plus runs rising over the next month.
- US "has to start to defend itself" within "a few weeks."
- Asia refinery turnarounds confirmed:
  - **SK Osan, Korea:** 260 KBD CDU + 66 KBD RFCC + 30 KBD reformer **down from May 20 for ~40 days**.
  - **Reliance Sika, India:** 660 KBD CDU + delayed coker **down 4 weeks**, starting after Niara refinery restarts in mid-May. Sika is serving domestic Indian market, so diesel exports continue.
- Goh refinery-economics caveat: turnaround schedules are typically locked in years ahead; postponing introduces process-safety risk; refineries CAN'T just stay running through.
- Indian domestic diesel prices are regulated/political; India may use tax-system tools to limit exports to cool domestic prices (similar to China's recent export-policy turnaround).

### Crude / OSPs / TMX / Japan (Ch 4)
- **Saudi June OSPs (released May 6):** Asia &minus;$4/bbl (vs market expectation of up to &minus;$8), Europe &minus;$2/bbl, US unchanged. "Saudi barrels look expensive right now."
- Spot crude alternatives to Aramco term: only Murban and Oman outside Australia. Upper Zakum traded at **OSP +$20/bbl X-Fujairah tanks** — quality premium (low TAN, low metals, base-oil/bitumen qualities). Quality of AG crude is "irreplaceable."
- Forties (40s) premium ~$180 yesterday — that's 2024-25 average level — even with Brent timespreads +$5. Quality is "underrated" piece of the story; Asians not willing to bid up 40s or WAF by +$10/bbl.
- **Iraqi SOMO Basra Medium offered FOB-$33/bbl discount** — but on FOB basis means buyer must enter Strait inbound (2 transits + likely IRGC payment). Small flow at best.
- **Japan: 12 million barrels of US crude locked in for August delivery** (WTI + Mars). Pre-crisis Japan was 1-5 mb/month. Crosby: locking in extreme US exports while US stocks draw → Aframax-to-NWE arb has to close → potential Brent signal. Crosby: "I've been at least a month early, probably two months early" on this thesis.
- Northwest Europe still well-supplied: 10-12 Afras of SPR-released US crude going to Europe; Mexican Olmeca and Maya into Europe (PMI refining assets); Norwegian + WAF diffs already wide enough.
- Crosby framing: "we're missing 15 million barrels per day" (late-Feb baseline) — taking longer than expected to filter through physical.
- **TMX pipeline tutorial (Goh):**
  - Nameplate 890 KBD; Westridge Marine Terminal nameplate 630 KBD; draft restriction limits to **~550 KB per Aframax cargo**.
  - Two export grades to Asia: **Cole Lake** and **High-Ten AWB**.
  - TMX could technically move lights/products but economics don't pencil. Run utilization heading to Asia (mainly China) is high.
  - Crisis-onset shift: **India and Singapore now picking up Canadian Aframaxes**, previously only Rang/ZPC China.
  - **Quality problem:** High-Ten AWB TAN = 1.86 (Cole Lake 1.32); Basra Medium TAN = 0.24. Refineries designed around middle-eastern crude have to **blend down to ~0.3 TAN** = roughly **10-15% blendstock**.
  - For every 550 KB Canadian cargo to Asia, need ~6M bbl (3 VLCCs) of low-TAN crude as blendstock — Goh: "they need lots and lots more WTI."
  - Constraint on how much TMX can absorb into Asia even though the barrels are available.

### Gasoline / Naphtha / GASNAP (Ch 5)
- TAR (transatlantic gasoline arb) keeps opening. Physical AR sending MR cargos ARA → PADD-1; prompt blender margin ~8¢/gal; June and July deliveries: numbers picking up. Open for refiners (not yet blenders).
- Post-3-year period of low margin even in summer; this summer expected to need higher TA than prior years to keep ARA→PADD-1 flow.
- Blending components rebounding (lagging stage is over), led by reformate uptrend, in both US and Europe.
- TC2 (UKC→USAC clean MR) Sparta dashboard prediction: **bullish** — transatlantic route demand picking up.
- East-West gasoline downtrend started Apr 8 ceasefire announcement; **now showing rebound** after one month falling. Will not reach the highs from a month and a half ago, but recovery has legs. Most E-of-Suez arbs still belong to Singapore.
- Europe still capturing LATAM gasoline destinations. EBOB strengthening preferentially short-term.
- E5 and E10 blend margins in ARA wildly open but Molinero says **largely ignore** when ARA has lots of export options (small parcel = pain in ass to fix).
- **Naphtha (different picture):** weaker paper time spreads, weaker cracks Europe + Asia, premiums in Japan/Korea/Taiwan still strong vs historical but well below war-peak. First half June arbs almost all closed from Europe; US north-coast and Algeria-switching barrels could trickle east.
- Premium "+$300" no longer accepted; wait-and-see mode after Asian inventories rebuilt. Cannot last — if AG NAFTA stays absent, premiums will need to rise and E/W will need to widen to reopen ARA→Asia.
- **GASNAP (gasoline-naphtha spread): historical highs** — bullish gasoline + weak naphtha. Translation: blending C5 / LPMS / LNs are supported looking forward; gap widening between blending grades and paraffinic/petrochemical qualities.
- 2022 summer historical highs still meaningfully above current — ceiling not confirmed yet.
